06 Data & assumptions

Reference-grounded prototype data

Port identities and broad operational characteristics are based on publicly available information from Indian port authorities and government/official sources. Freight rates, congestion, waiting time, fuel assumptions and cost values are synthetic demonstration data.

Prototype uses reference-grounded synthetic data for demonstration. Operational, freight and congestion values are simulated and are not live commercial data. Draft figures are approximate reference assumptions and can vary by berth, tide, season and vessel condition.
